Testing · quality engineering

I'm a tester.

Not QA. Not quality assurance. A tester. It isn't a job I clock into — it's how I'm wired.

01 How I'm wired

I poke at the thing everyone assumes is fine and find the defect nobody wanted found — in a codebase, in a plan, in a "fact" someone stated a little too confidently. People state things blindly; I love asking is that actually true? and watching what happens. Same with assumptions — especially the load-bearing ones nobody thought to check.

Доверяй, но проверяй — trust, but verify. I test to see, not to confirm. Even when I'm certain I know the answer, I'd rather watch it happen than trust the theory. Don't take the claim — check the thing. Seeing beats assuming.

02 From QA to QE

You cannot inspect quality into a product. That's not the objection — it's the point. If you can't inspect it in, you build it in.

QA was reactive: validate at the end, run the tests, find the bugs after the work was already done. Quality Engineering is proactive — build the architecture and the feedback loops that keep defects from being written in the first place, and catch the ones that slip the moment they land. I stopped guarding the border between "testing" and "engineering" and started owning both sides of it.

What I own isn't assuring quality — I don't control the schedule or the scope, and I've made my peace with that. What I own is the system that lets the organization build quality in, and the information that makes the real state of it impossible to fake.

03 AI, held to the same bar

I run models on my own hardware. I'm not theorizing about AI — I'm using it every day, and building with it, not waiting for it: AI-assisted test generation, agent-based testing, defect diagnostics.

The AI handles the mechanical work; the tester decides what matters. So I own the evaluation discipline and the human-in-the-loop governance that holds AI-assisted work to the same bar as anything written by hand. Trust the agent to do the work; verify it exactly as hard. An agent is a powerful new instrument. It is not an excuse to stop thinking.

04 The measurement loop

Measure everything — but the right things. Quality is value to someone who matters, not a number on a dashboard: measure outcomes and conversion, not vanity volume. Read the numbers to learn, never as a target to hit — the moment a metric becomes a goal, it gets gamed. The analytics aren't decoration; they're the proof. Доверяй, но проверяй.

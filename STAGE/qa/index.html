QA · testing · quality engineering

Quality is the job.

Not a phase at the end, not someone else's problem — the thing you build in from the first line. This is the part of the work that's most me.

01 How I think about it

I started as a tester and never lost the instinct. The tester's job isn't to catch bugs at the door — it's to make the whole system harder to get wrong: better contracts, better feedback loops, better defaults, so the failure mode stops happening instead of getting caught.

I care about measuring quality honestly — a green checkmark that isn't evidence is worse than a red one. If a test can pass while the thing is broken, the test is the bug. Instrument everything; trust the output, not the exit code.

And I care about leaving the people after me better equipped: repeatable process over heroics, documented decisions over tribal knowledge.

03 Principles I hold

Measure it, or it didn't happen. Every feature ships with the metric that proves it works.

Fix the gap, keep the bar. When something falls short, raise the system — don't lower the standard.

Don't reduce judgment to a checkbox. Quality is value delivered, measured for insight — not vanity dashboards.

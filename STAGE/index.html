01 About

I'm a tester who ended up building things — self-taught, practical, and I'd rather show you than tell you. I don't trust marketing or demos; I trust what happens when I put something in front of a real problem and watch whether it holds up. Try it, break it, decide.

I lean build, and I run it on my own hardware — not out of ego, but control. When you own the thing, you understand it; when it breaks, you can fix it. That instinct turned a spare machine in a closet into a small fleet, and a hobby into the way I work.

Honesty over harmony, output over hours, trust over credentials.

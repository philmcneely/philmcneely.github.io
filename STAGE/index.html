Homelab tinkerer turned self-hosted-AI builder — tester at heart

I'm a homelab enthusiast who fell into AI the deep-end way: self-hosting local LLMs for the fun of it, then building real systems around them. A box in a closet became a few more; tinkering turned into engineering. I try to do it well — find the repeatable better way, build quality in from the start — and share what I learn in the open.

01 About

It started the way these things do — a spare machine, a closet, and curiosity. I've always been a tester at heart, happier finding the repeatable better way than shipping fast and sorry.

When local LLMs got genuinely good, I was hooked: self-hosting models, then wiring them into pipelines, then building a whole software factory around them. That home lab is now the Draconis fleet — a handful of machines and GPUs humming in the background — but it's still, at bottom, a place to build things properly and learn out loud.

Honesty over harmony, output over hours, trust over credentials.
